# DSS User Evaluation Plan

This plan is a template only. Do not claim user-evaluation results until real participants have completed the study and any required university ethics/approval process has been satisfied.

## Suggested participant tasks
1. Select a factory and generate a supported next-month price forecast.
2. Enter an expected green-leaf quantity and interpret the estimated gross earnings.
3. Interpret the approximate prediction range and reliability level.
4. Use the factory-comparison screen to identify the highest predicted Reasonable Price alternatives.
5. Open a selected factory and inspect the SHAP-based explanation.
6. Explain why the system does not guarantee that the highest-price factory is the objectively best choice.

## Suggested measures
- task completion success;
- time on task;
- user-reported ease of use;
- clarity of price and earnings output;
- clarity of uncertainty/reliability information;
- perceived usefulness for planning;
- trust calibrated to the stated limitations;
- SUS, if approved/appropriate for the module.

## Required research integrity
- use actual participants;
- use informed consent where required;
- avoid unnecessary personal information;
- anonymize/aggregate reported responses;
- report negative and positive feedback;
- do not fabricate survey values or interviews.
